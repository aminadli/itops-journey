## Preface: The Situation

I joined a small company as the sole IT person. The environment was legacy and unmanaged: manual processes, no documentation, no monitoring, no backups. There was no budget for commercial tooling like Intune, Entra ID, Jira, Splunk, or Veeam.

Using the knowledge and concept that i learn from MD 102, Net+, Sec+ and Linux+,I decided to build a modern, self-hosted, Docker-based IT Operations platform. The constraints:

- **Solo admin** — no team to help
- **Limited resources** — one VM, modest RAM and disk
- **No budget** — open-source only
- **Legacy environment** — migrate from manual workflows
- **"Set and forget"** — low maintenance, high reliability

This journal documents the build, including every difficulty, to avoid making the same mistakes.

## 🇲🇾 Project Mission & Context
I believe that many **SMEs in Malaysia** face significant budget constraints when dealing with IT infrastructure management. Proprietary IT management software can be financially out of reach for growing local businesses. 

By documenting this lightweight, self-hosted, and open-source Docker deployment, I hope to provide a reliable blueprint to help fellow Malaysian IT enthusiasts, systems administrators, and SMEs deploy a complete IT Operation Management System Stack without the enterprise price tag.
